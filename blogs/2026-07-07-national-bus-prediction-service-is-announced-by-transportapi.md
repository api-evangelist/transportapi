07 Jul 2026 by Jonathan Raper TransportAPI announces the opening of the National Bus Prediction Service, which will provide bus RTI for all bus stops in Great Britain. The new NBPS will build on the NextBuses - powered by TransportAPI and extend bus RTI to rural stops.
