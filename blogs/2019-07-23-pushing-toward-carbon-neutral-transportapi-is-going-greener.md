23 Jul 2019 by Angus Hanton Better journey planning is at the heart of what TransportAPI does, and this also makes transport more sustainable. It helps people to use the shortest route but also tends to shift people away from cars towards greener options of shared, public transport. On a system-wide level our data helps councils and transport companies to plan more efficient routes and time-tabling.
